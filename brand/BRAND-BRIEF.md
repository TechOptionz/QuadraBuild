# Quadra Build — brand brief for logo and identity work

Background for designing more logos and brand assets that match the Quadra Build website (quadra-build-beta.vercel.app). Use it together with the logo files and `README.md` in this kit.

## Suggested prompt

> Using the attached Quadra Build logo files, README and this brief, design a set of logo variations for the brand: division lockups for the four divisions, one-colour versions in ink and paper, a simplified small-size icon and favicon, and a few social and signage applications. Keep the existing Q-House mark and its diagonal Q tail, and stay within the colours and type described here.

## The business

- **Name:** Quadra Build
- **What it does:** Renovations, new homes, commercial fit-outs and development approvals support, under one brand and one coordinated team.
- **Where:** Brisbane-based, working across Brisbane and South East Queensland (SEQ), Australia.
- **Who it's for:** Homeowners, businesses and property owners. The work is mainly residential, with a commercial arm.
- **The core idea:** Projects rarely fit one box. A renovation may need approvals, and a new home may involve outdoor living. Quadra brings four specialist divisions together so clients get one consistent standard instead of piecing together separate providers.
- **Parent brand:** "Quadra Build Group" has been floated but is **not confirmed**, so don't design for it yet.

## Personality and voice

- **Values (from the About page):** Clarity, Craft, Coordination, Accountability.
- **What clients should expect:** clear communication, tailored solutions, coordinated services, attention to detail, local understanding.
- **Tone:** confident, plain-spoken, grounded and practical. Premium but not flashy, and local rather than corporate.
- **Lines used on the site:**
  - "We Build. We Renovate. We Transform." (homepage hero)
  - "Building Better Spaces. Creating Better Futures." (tagline)
  - "One Team. Multiple Ways to Build Better."
  - "This page isn't part of the plan." (404 page; a little dry wit is on-brand)
- **Process:** Discover, Assess, Plan, Build, Complete.

## Visual system on the website

- **Colours:**
  - Warm paper `#F3F0E9` background
  - Ink `#1C1D1A` text
  - Graphite `#1D1E1B` dark sections
  - Terracotta/brick `#A4533A` as the single main accent; `#E48A5C` is its brighter version on dark backgrounds
  - The four division colours above
- **Type:** Archivo (bold, slightly widened, tight tracking) for display and the wordmark. Instrument Sans for body text.
- **Shape language:** square corners, almost no shadows, thin rules and dividers, big confident type, and a lot of whitespace.
- **Motifs:**
  - Fine architectural grid lines
  - Numbered items such as "01 / 02 / 03"
  - Small coloured squares as division keys
  - Spec-sheet style labels such as "SUBURB · SCOPE · DURATION"
- **Photography direction:**
  - South East Queensland light: warm late afternoon, timber battens, deep eaves, Queenslanders beside contemporary homes
  - Residential crews rather than industrial safety gear
  - One warm, slightly muted grade across every image
- **Creative direction from the brand audit, "Four parts, one build":** the four-block logo becomes the whole visual system. Each division owns one block colour, and the homepage shows all four together.

## What to design next (ideas from the audit)

1. **Division lockups**, such as "QUADRA | Renovations", with the divider (or a block) in that division's colour.
2. **One-colour versions** in ink and in paper, for signage, stamps, embroidery, site hoardings and vehicles.
3. **A small-size icon and favicon.** The shaded 3D edges get muddy below about 32px, so a simpler version is needed for tiny sizes.
4. **Social avatar** and **email signature** versions.
5. **Applications:** site signage and hoarding, hi-vis or polo embroidery, vehicle livery, business cards, document and quote headers.
6. **Optionally, a monogram/Q-only mark** for very tight spaces.

## Guardrails

- **Keep the Q-House mark** and especially the **diagonal Q tail**. That's what makes the house read as a Q, and the owner wants to keep it.
- Keep **QUADRA BUILD on one line**, with BUILD lighter than QUADRA.
- Watch that the tail never reads as a **magnifying glass handle**, which suggests a property-search app rather than a builder.
- On dark backgrounds, **lighten the slate** (`#C3C9CF` face, `#7D858D` edge) so it doesn't disappear.
- Avoid construction clichés: hard hats, hammers, cranes and generic roof swooshes.
- Don't borrow other builders' identities.
- **Don't invent facts.** That means no licence numbers, years established, awards, phone numbers or "since 19xx" lines. Use obvious placeholders such as "QBCC 0000000" in mock-ups.
- Text in sage or sandstone fails contrast, so use the darker text versions listed above.
